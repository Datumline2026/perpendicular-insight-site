Founder

William Eric Black founded Perpendicular Insight after more than thirteen years at Credit Suisse, where he was Managing Director, Head of Counterparty & CCP Risk Management, leading a team across the US, Singapore and the UK and advising senior leadership and the U.S. Board of Directors. Before that he was Global Head of Product for OTC Client Clearing — owning the product roadmap and governance for an $80M+ revenue franchise across rates, credit and FX.

The work spanned roughly 40 CCP memberships, ~$160B of initial margin and 1,500+ client relationships, through events including the Archegos response, the ISDA 2021 Definitions rollout and the UBS integration. Earlier: Markit (VP, Rates Product Management) and Fannie Mae's derivatives desk.

He has served on the boards of ISDA and FIA, and on the ForexClear (LCH) board.
